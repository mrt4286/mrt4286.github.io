Bursa Food Guide

Bursa, the first Ottoman capital, is famous for İskender kebab, chestnut candy and Mount Uludağ.

İskender kebab

By tradition, İskender kebab was first served in 1867 by Mehmetoğlu İskender Efendi of Bursa. Sliced meat cooked on a horizontal spit is served over pide with butter, tomato sauce and yogurt; it is also called Bursa kebab. This story comes from oral tradition, and we found no primary documentation.

Bursa döner kebab and pideli köfte

Bursa döner kebab and Bursa pideli köfte have received geographical indication registration. Bursa has more than thirty registered local products.

Chestnut candy

Bursa chestnut candy (kestane şekeri) is geographically protected in Türkiye and registered in the European Union. It is the city's signature gift sweet.

More to taste

Cantık, tahinli pide, walnut Turkish delight, black figs, Gemlik olives and Bursa peaches are also well-known products; the last three are EU-registered too.

Frequently asked questions

Where did İskender kebab originate?

By tradition, in Bursa in 1867 with Mehmetoğlu İskender Efendi; sources pass this on as oral tradition.

Is Bursa chestnut candy protected?

Yes, with a Turkish geographical indication and EU registration.

What should I eat in Bursa?

İskender, pideli köfte, cantık, tahinli pide and chestnut candy.
