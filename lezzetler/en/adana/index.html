Adana Food Guide

Adana, the kebab and turnip-juice capital of Çukurova, is home to one of Türkiye's iconic dishes.

Adana kebab

Adana kebab received a geographical indication registration through the Adana Chamber of Commerce. Spicy minced meat is shaped onto a skewer and grilled over charcoal, served with lavash, onions and roasted vegetables. In one survey it ranked third among the geographically protected foods people think of first, after Gaziantep baklava and Malatya apricots.

Şalgam

The fermented turnip and carrot drink served with kebab, şalgam, is also protected for Adana. The city is closely tied to kebab and şalgam.

Other registered foods

Adana kol böreği, Adana içli köfte, Adana taş kadayıfı and Karataş kıddesi are also registered. The city's table is rich in pastries and desserts too.

On the Seyhan

The Seyhan River flows through the middle of the city, and food and social life meet along its banks.

What do you drink with Adana kebab?

Şalgam, a turnip drink; Adana şalgam is protected as well.
