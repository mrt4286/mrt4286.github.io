Hatay Food Guide

Hatay, where civilizations meet, is known for its künefe and a rich meze table.

A UNESCO City of Gastronomy

Hatay was admitted to UNESCO's Creative Cities Network for gastronomy in 2017, after Gaziantep. Where the Mediterranean meets Anatolia, hundreds of local dishes are counted in its cuisine.

Antakya künefe

Künefe received a geographical indication registration in 2008. A special unsalted, stretchy cheese is placed between strands of fine kadayıf, fried in butter and finished with syrup. The stretch of the cheese when served hot is its best-known feature.

Paper kebab and mezes

Kağıt kebabı (paper kebab) is also among Hatay's registered foods. A Hatay table brings together mezes, several kebabs and local pastries.

Cultural variety

Hatay cuisine carries Arab, Turkish and Mediterranean influences, which makes it both rich and distinctive.
