Why you'll love it

81 provinces, 81 flavors

Ten levels in every province, with the region's own treats and scenery. Taste Anatolia from Afyon clotted cream to Gaziantep baklava.

Made in Türkiye

Every flavor, landscape and story is inspired by Turkish culture: baklava, Turkish delight, simit, tea, the evil-eye bead and more.

Evliya Çelebi by your side

The famous 17th-century traveler guides you across the map and introduces the culture of each region.

Infirmary levels

Help Ibn Sina in sliding puzzles: gather herbs and brew syrups (fictional, not medical advice).

Combos and power-ups

Create giant combos with the teapot, evil-eye bead, hot-air balloon and Rooster Candy. Shuffle, Hammer and extra-move boosters help you out.

Free, in Turkish and English

The game is free to download and contains ads. Available in English and Turkish.

Frequently asked questions

What is Patlayan Lezzetler?

Patlayan Lezzetler ("Popping Flavors") is a free match puzzle game where you line up three or more identical treats. It is themed around Türkiye's 81 provinces and their regional foods.

Is it free?

Yes, it is free to download. The game contains ads; watching rewarded ads is optional.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones via Google Play, and iPhone and iPad via the App Store.

Which languages are supported?

English and Turkish.
