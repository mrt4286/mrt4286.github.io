Patlayan Lezzetler Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Applies to: Patlayan Lezzetler (Android and iOS)

This policy explains which data is processed while you play Patlayan Lezzetler, why, and what your rights are. Data controller: Murat Erdemir (individual developer) — contact: muraterdemir42@gmail.com.

In short: There are no accounts. We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts. Your game progress is stored only on your own device. The game shows Google AdMob ads; personalizing ads depends only on your permission.

1. What data do we process?

DataPurpose
Game progress (unlocked provinces, stars, sugar crumbs, power-ups, settings, chosen language)Resuming the game where you left off. Stored only on your device; it is not sent to us.
Advertising data: advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address), device and app information — only while ads are shown, by Google AdMobShowing and measuring ads (see 3)
Crash reports (Android only, Google Firebase Crashlytics): crash trace, device model, OS versionFinding and fixing bugs. Contains no personal data. No crash reports are collected on iOS.

We do not collect accounts, logins, email, name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data or payment information. There are no purchases with real money in the game and no chat or user-generated content.

2. Why and on what basis?

  • Providing and maintaining the game (saving progress, fixing bugs): performance of a contract and legitimate interest.
  • Showing ads: your explicit consent for ad personalization (the tracking permission prompt on iPhone, Google's consent form in the EEA and UK). You can withdraw consent at any time.

3. Ads and permissions

The game shows Google AdMob ads: optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads between levels. Google's consent form is shown in the EEA and UK, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt on iPhone. If you do not allow tracking, ads are shown without personalization and the game works the same. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalization in your device settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Who do we share data with?

We do not sell your data. Only these providers are involved:

  • Google AdMob (advertising).
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (Android only, crash reports).
  • Apple and Google (app stores): downloads and updates are subject to their own policies.

These providers may process data on servers outside Türkiye; this transfer is inherent to the service and the providers' own privacy safeguards apply.

5. How long do we keep data?

  • Your game progress is on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • We hold no personal data on our servers. Advertising and crash data are subject to the retention periods of the providers.

6. Your rights

Under KVKK (Law No. 6698, Art. 11) and, where applicable, GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, object to processing and withdraw consent. Because we hold no account-linked personal data, most requests are met by uninstalling the app. For other requests write to muraterdemir42@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your national data protection authority (KVKK Board for Türkiye).

7. Children

Patlayan Lezzetler is not specifically directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad content is limited by rating (general audience with parental guidance). A parent who thinks a child has shared data can write to muraterdemir42@gmail.com.

8. Medical information notice

The Infirmary, syrups and herbs in the game are entirely fictional. The game gives no medical information, diagnosis or treatment advice.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: muraterdemir42@gmail.com.
